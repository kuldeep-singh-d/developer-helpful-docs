# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, patient, and welcoming. Discuss ideas and technical details without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Expected behavior

- Give feedback that is specific and helpful.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Correct mistakes without embarrassing contributors.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the repository.
- Protect private and sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, doxxing, spam, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an appropriate GitHub contact method. Do not publish sensitive incident details in a public issue.
